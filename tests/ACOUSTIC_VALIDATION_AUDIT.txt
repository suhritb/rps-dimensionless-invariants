ACOUSTIC VALIDATION AUDIT — ORIGINAL FLAT-ERROR RESULT
=======================================================

Recorded during solver development, after compressible_solver.py was first written.

Setup
-----
  Solver  : src/compressible_solver.py (pseudo-spectral RK4)
  IC      : make_acoustic_ic — inviscid isentropic (Re->inf) eigenvector
  Method  : two-point estimate sigma = log(A0/A_T) / T_final
  Reference: exact_acoustic_decay_rate — cubic dispersion polynomial eigenvalue

Diagnosis
---------
The isentropic IC uses the Re->inf eigenvector.  At finite Re the exact
right-going eigenvector differs by O(1/Re), causing a backward-mode projection
of magnitude |c_backward|/|c_forward| ~ 1.83e-3 for Re=200.  Both acoustic
modes have exactly the same real part of their eigenvalue (same decay rate
sigma), so the beat between them persists indefinitely.  The velocity amplitude
is not a pure exponential but an exponential multiplied by a sinusoid at
frequency 2*k/Ma.  A two-point estimate at fixed times samples two arbitrary
phases of this beat and carries a systematic bias of O(1/Re).

Pure-eigenmode + multipoint LS fit result (same Re=200, Ma=0.3)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  N=64   rel_err=5.59e-06
  N=128  rel_err=2.42e-06
  N=256  rel_err=6.98e-07
  Convergence order ~1.8 (solver truncation now dominates, not measurement bias)

Conclusion
----------
The solver is correct.  The 2.714e-3 error was entirely a measurement artifact.
Fix applied: validate_acoustic_decay now uses make_eigenmode_ic (exact finite-Re
eigenvector) and a 40-point least-squares exponential fit.  Tolerance 1e-4 is
met with margin ~18x.  No tolerance change was made.
